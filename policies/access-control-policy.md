# Access Control Policy

**Company:** Example Co. (fictional)
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Ervan, IT Security
**Last reviewed:** 10/23/2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for who can access Example Co. systems and data, and how that access is granted, reviewed, and removed.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and interns, and to all company systems, applications, and data.

## 3. Access Principles

3.1 **Least privilege:** Users receive only the access they need to do their job.

3.2 **Need to know:** Access to sensitive data (such as customer or financial records) requires approval from the data owner.

3.3 **Unique accounts:** Every user has their own account. Shared or generic accounts are not allowed unless approved by IT Security and documented.

## 4. Granting Access

4.1 Access requests must be submitted in writing (for example, through a ticket) and approved by the user's manager.

4.2 Access is based on job role. Each role has a standard set of permissions.

4.3 All access requests and approvals are recorded.

## 5. Privileged Access

5.1 Administrator accounts are used only for administrative tasks, and not for everyday work like email or browsing.

5.2 Administrator accounts require MFA and must be approved by IT Security.

5.3 Administrator activity is logged and reviewed monthly.

## 6. Access Reviews

6.1 Managers review their team's access every 6 months and confirm it is still needed.

6.2 Access that is no longer needed is removed within 5 business days of the review.

## 7. Changes and Departures

7.1 When an employee changes roles, their old access is removed and new access is granted based on the new role.

7.2 When an employee or contractor leaves, HR notifies IT, and all access is disabled on their last day.

## 8. Remote Access

8.1 Remote access to company systems must use the company VPN and MFA.

8.2 Remote access from personal devices is allowed only for approved applications.

## 9. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 10. Review

This policy is reviewed once a year, or sooner if there is a major security incident or change in technology.
