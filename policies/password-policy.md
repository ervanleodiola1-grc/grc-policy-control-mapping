# Password and Authentication Policy

**Company:** Example Co. (fictional)
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** [Your name], IT Security
**Last reviewed:** [Date]

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for creating, using, and protecting passwords so that only authorized people can access Example Co. systems and data.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and interns who use company accounts, devices, or applications.

## 3. Password Requirements

3.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.

3.2 Longer passphrases (for example, four random words) are encouraged over short, complex passwords.

3.3 Passwords must not be reused across different accounts, and must not be the same as personal account passwords.

3.4 Passwords must be checked against a list of commonly used or previously breached passwords, and rejected if they appear on it.

3.5 Passwords must be changed immediately if there is any sign they have been compromised. Regular forced changes are not required.

## 4. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

4.1 MFA is required for email, VPN, cloud applications, and all administrator accounts.

4.2 Authenticator apps or hardware keys are preferred. SMS codes should only be used if no better option is available.

## 5. Password Storage and Sharing

5.1 Employees must use a company-approved password manager to store passwords.

5.2 Passwords must never be shared with anyone, including IT staff or managers, or written on paper or in unprotected files.

5.3 Systems must store passwords only as salted hashes, never in plain text.

## 6. Account Lockout

6.1 Accounts are temporarily locked after 5 failed login attempts.

6.2 Locked accounts are unlocked after 15 minutes or by contacting the IT help desk after identity verification.

## 7. Reporting

Anyone who suspects a password has been exposed must report it to IT Security right away so the account can be secured.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed once a year, or sooner if there is a major security incident or change in technology.
